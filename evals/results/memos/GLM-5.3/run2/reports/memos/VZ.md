   ✅ supported (judge): All claim components match the computed scorecard values: SB-4 grade, 3.90 weighted score with 1 strongest, stylized not a credit rating, and not provisional since all six factors were scored.
2. The strongest-scoring factor is interest coverage, which sits in band **3** [F37] and carries the heaviest weight at **25.0%** [F38], reflecting EBITDA / interest of **6.36x** [F13] in the twelve months to 30 September 2026.  
   ✅ supported (judge): Metric values confirm interest coverage is band 3 with the heaviest weight at 25.0% (all other weights are 20% or lower) and EBITDA/interest of 6.36x for the LTM to 30 September 2026.
3. The weakest-scoring factor is liquidity coverage, in band **5** [F39] with a weight of **15.0%** [F40] and measured at **1.06x** [F20] in the twelve months to 30 September 2026, while near-term maturity cover sits in band **4** [F41] with a weight of **10.0%** [F42] and stands at **0.86x** [F33] in the fiscal year ended 31 December 2025.  
   ✅ supported (judge): All claim components match the metric values: liquidity coverage band 5, weight 15.0%, 1.06x LTM to 30 Sep 2026; near-term maturity cover band 4, weight 10.0%, 0.86x FY ended 31 Dec 2025.
4. The remaining factors fall in middling bands: debt / EBITDA of **3.42x** [F7] scores in band **4** [F43] with a weight of **20.0%** [F44], net leverage of **3.33x** [F8] scores in band **4** [F45] with a weight of **10.0%** [F46], and FCF / debt of **13.5%** [F16] scores in band **4** [F47] with a weight of **20.0%** [F48].  
   ✅ supported (judge): All three factor values, bands, and weights match the scorecard evidence exactly.
5. Earlier findings support the grade at this level: Verizon is one of the world's leading providers of communications, technology, information and streaming products and services, and management believes its high-quality networks and customer base support its competitive position and its ability to manage through changing market conditions, which underpins comparatively stable cash flows.
